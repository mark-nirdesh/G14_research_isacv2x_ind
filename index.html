Research at a glance

One USRP B210 acts as a stationary sensing roadside node. It transmits a custom 5.9 GHz V2X-inspired OFDM waveform containing data and known reference symbols, and records reflected RF signals. A second B210 independently receives the communication packets.

We will investigate whether a moving target can be detected despite direct transmitter leakage and static roadside clutter, while measuring packet error rate and goodput at the separate receiver. Target-detection results are not yet claimed.

This is an experimental prototype, not a claim of full ETSI ITS-G5 or 3GPP PC5 compliance. Over-the-air transmissions require appropriate authorization.
